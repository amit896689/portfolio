01 · Profile

Remote sensing scientist, engineer of geo-information

I am a doctoral researcher in the 39th cycle of the National PhD in Earth Observation, jointly enrolled at Sapienza University of Rome and the University of Naples Federico II. My thesis develops machine-learning frameworks that convert Copernicus Earth Observation data into ready-to-use Land Use/Land Cover thematic products.

From April to September 2026 I join the School of Civil & Environmental Engineering at Cornell University as an ISSNAF Visiting PhD Scholar, building crop-mapping models that support irrigation water and energy optimisation in critical infrastructure systems.

Before my PhD I contributed to ISRO- and SERB-funded projects on landslide monitoring, river-to-ocean discharge estimation and construction-material durability across IIT Roorkee, IIT Delhi, IIT Bombay and NIT Agartala — and I ranked 1st in my M.Tech cohort (Gold Medal, CGPA 8.79/10).

My work sits at the intersection of remote sensing, hydrology and machine learning: from mapping irrigated cropland in Southern Italy and monitoring rice growth stages with harmonised Sentinel-2 & SAR data, to flood-hazard assessment and hybrid deep-learning landslide detection.
